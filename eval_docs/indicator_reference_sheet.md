# Indicator reference sheet

One of these per indicator. It is the document that stops two people
computing the same indicator two ways, and it is usually written after that
has already happened. Write it before.

`indicator_reference_sheet.csv` holds the same fields for a whole indicator
set in one file.

## The sheet

| Field | What goes here | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Indicator name** | Short, as it appears in the logframe | Share of girls reporting use of a sanitary product |
| **Logframe level and row** | Which objective it measures | Outcome 1 |
| **Precise definition** | The full sentence, with every term defined | Percentage of girls enrolled in classes 6 to 8 in programme schools who report, when asked in the endline survey, having used a commercially produced or programme-distributed sanitary pad during their most recent menstrual period |
| **Numerator** | Exactly who is counted | Girls in classes 6 to 8 who answered "pad" to Q14 |
| **Denominator** | Exactly who is the base | Girls in classes 6 to 8 who answered Q13 "yes" (have begun menstruating) and were surveyed at endline |
| **Unit** | Percentage, count, rupees, days | Percentage |
| **Disaggregation** | Every breakdown that will be reported | Class; school; whether the girl attended at least three sessions |
| **Data source** | The instrument and the question number | Endline survey, Q13 and Q14 |
| **Collection method** | Who asks, how, of whom | Trained female enumerator, face to face, in a private room, self-report |
| **Frequency** | When it is measured | Baseline (July), endline (March) |
| **Responsible** | A role, not a name | MEL officer |
| **Baseline** | Value, date, source | 38%, July 2024, baseline survey |
| **Target** | Value and date | 60%, March 2025 |
| **Direction** | Higher is better, or lower | Higher |
| **Known limitations** | What this number cannot tell you | Self-reported to programme staff; expect a demand effect; excludes girls absent on survey day, who are more likely to be the girls the programme is about |
| **Calculation notes** | Weighting, exclusions, edge cases | Unweighted; girls who have not begun menstruating are excluded from both numerator and denominator, not counted as zero |

## The three fields that matter most

**Precise definition.** Every word that could be read two ways is defined.
"Uses a sanitary product" becomes a sentence naming the products, the
reference period and the source of the report. If the definition changes
between rounds, the indicator is not comparable across rounds, and the sheet
is where the change is recorded.

**Denominator.** More indicators are wrong in the denominator than in the
numerator. Girls who have not begun menstruating: excluded, or counted as
not using a product? Households that could not be found at endline: in the
denominator or out? The sheet answers it once.

**Known limitations.** Written by the person who designed the measure, for
the person who will quote it in a report without having read anything else.
It is the only field that protects the number from being used to say more
than it can.
