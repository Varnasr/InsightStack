Interviewer: Can you tell me how the heat has affected your outreach?

CHW Rekha: Yes, it’s difficult now. We can’t go after 9:30 in the morning. I feel faint even before I reach the first household.

Interviewer: What do people say when you reach their homes?

CHW Rekha: They often ask us to come in the evening or wait under the tree nearby. Everyone is scared of heatstroke this year.

Interviewer: Have you had to change your routine?

CHW Rekha: I try to finish more in the early hours. But then, fewer people are awake and willing to talk. It's a problem either way.
