When I walk through the fields now, it's quiet — not because work is done, but because no one can bear the sun after 10AM.

In the interviews, women tell me how they now fetch water at 4AM. Some men stay indoors all day. Livelihoods are changing — not slowly, but suddenly.

One woman asked me if this is what climate change means. I didn’t have a good answer, but I wrote down her words carefully.
