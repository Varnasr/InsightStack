# Annotated Research Briefs

This folder includes a sample annotated research note to help users understand how research design decisions, methods, and limitations are communicated in development sector outputs.

## 📄 File Included

- `annotated_research_brief_mhh.pdf`: A real-style 3-page research note on menstrual health and school attendance in Jharkhand, with inline annotations.

## 🧠 Why Use This?

Annotated briefs are useful for:
- Teaching how to critically read and interpret rapid assessments or evaluations
- Training early-career researchers or MEL professionals
- Demonstrating framing, methods, and transparency in reporting

## 🛠️ Suggestions

- Use for brown-bag or internal learning sessions
- Create your own versions with comments explaining MEL, qualitative insights, sampling strategies, and more
