
# Using Miro in a Real-World Indian Field Context

## 🧾 Case Title: Mapping Frontline Health Gaps in Khunti, Jharkhand

In early 2023, a participatory Theory of Change (ToC) session was organized in Khunti district, Jharkhand, with IHCRF fellows, local NGO staff, and district ASHA supervisors. The goal was to jointly identify bottlenecks in maternal health access and visualize realistic solutions.

Instead of using whiteboards or chart paper, the facilitation team used Miro to structure the conversation into zones: “Barriers to Care,” “Local Workarounds,” “Missed Groups,” and “Desired Outcomes.”

Participants were first walked through how to use sticky notes on Miro, with a warm-up prompt: “Write down one thing that’s working well in your area.” This built confidence. Once the session began, live sticky storming was used to surface issues such as delayed incentive payments, overburdened frontline workers, and supply-side gaps in antenatal care.

Using Miro’s color-coded notes (yellow for gaps, blue for opportunities), the group was able to cluster insights and draw arrows between recurring root causes. One ASHA noted that "Incentives delayed for three months" linked to both "low motivation" and "incomplete home visits." That causal logic was preserved visually—something rarely captured in verbal-only workshops.

By the end, the board included four clear clusters and a visual ToC that the block-level planning unit adopted into their next quarterly strategy. The exported Miro board (PDF + PNG) was printed and shared in Hindi with community partners.

## 🔍 Why Miro Worked

- **Remote prep**: The board was created collaboratively by the facilitators in advance, saving time.
- **Hybrid-ready**: Those unable to use Miro participated via WhatsApp or phone while facilitators added their inputs.
- **Transparency**: The visual trace of discussion allowed revisiting any idea or missed voice post-session.

## 🧠 Key Tip

Prep anchor participants (e.g., one fellow and one ASHA) ahead of time to model confidence in using the board during the live session.
