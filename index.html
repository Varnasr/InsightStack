DHS, South Asia

One loader for every DHS in the region, from India's NFHS-1 to NFHS-5 through Bangladesh, Nepal, Pakistan, Maldives, Afghanistan and Sri Lanka. Applies the weight scaling, the design variables and the anthropometry flags.
