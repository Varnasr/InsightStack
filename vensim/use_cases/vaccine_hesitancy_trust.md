# Vaccine Hesitancy and Trust Dynamics

## Context
This model simulates how misinformation, trust in public health institutions, and community education efforts dynamically influence vaccine uptake in a population.

## Key Variables
- **Trust in Public Health**: Confidence in health authorities.
- **Misinformation Spread**: Rate at which falsehoods propagate.
- **Community Education**: Counter-efforts to strengthen trust.
- **Vaccine Uptake**: Final behavioral outcome driven by trust.

## Feedback Loops
- **Negative Loop**: Misinformation → declining trust → lower vaccine uptake.
- **Positive Loop**: Education campaigns → improved trust → increased vaccine uptake.

## Policy Relevance
Useful for planning public health communication strategies, understanding challenges in mass immunization drives (polio, COVID-19), and targeting misinformation control efforts.

## Region/Application
COVID-19 vaccine hesitancy pockets in rural India, routine immunization struggles in tribal belts, global south polio campaign setbacks.
