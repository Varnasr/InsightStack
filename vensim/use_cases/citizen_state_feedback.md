# Citizen-State Welfare Feedback Loop

## Context
This model simulates how citizen trust, service access, and grievance redress mechanisms dynamically reinforce or erode governance outcomes in welfare delivery systems.

## Key Variables
- **Service Access**: Availability and quality of government services.
- **Citizen Trust**: Trust in state institutions.
- **Grievance Redress**: Mechanism effectiveness in resolving citizen complaints.

## Feedback Loops
- **Positive Loop**: Improved access → higher trust → more engagement → better governance.
- **Negative Loop**: Poor redress → trust erosion → disengagement → worsened service outcomes.

## Policy Relevance
Relevant for designing social audits, grievance portals, public service guarantee acts, and participatory accountability mechanisms like RTI-based grievance systems.

## Region/Application
MGNREGA transparency initiatives, Rajasthan's Right to Public Services Act, digital grievance redress systems in Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka.
